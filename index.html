What this score means

NoGPT v1 is an interpretable heuristic, not a trained or benchmark-calibrated AI detector. It combines sentence-length uniformity, repeated word sequences, formulaic transitions, lexical diversity, repeated openings, and consistent cadence into a 0–100 writing-pattern score. It cannot determine authorship, identify a particular model, or measure a probability that text is AI-generated. Short, formal, translated, non-English, heavily edited, or technical writing can be especially misleading. For meaningful review, analyze at least 150–200 words and inspect the underlying signals. All analyses run on this device. Original documents and scores exist in this tab's memory only and disappear when you close/reload the page.

Do not use these results as sole evidence of AI use in school, employment, publishing, or any consequential setting.
